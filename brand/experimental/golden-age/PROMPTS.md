# Card Hunt golden-age monkey: image prompts

Three different characters and compositions. Run each prompt in an image generator
(ChatGPT/DALL·E, Midjourney, Firefly, Ideogram...). Please make:
1. the main illustration, landscape 5:4, as big as the tool allows
2. the same character alone on a plain dark background, square (for the mascot and icon)

Leave out text, borders and card frames (Blender and the compositor add those).
Don't name real artists or real cards in the prompt. The goal is the era's look, not a copy.

Shared style line (add it to every prompt):
> traditional hand-painted fantasy illustration in the style of early-1990s fantasy trading cards and
> paperback book covers, oil and acrylic on board, visible brushwork, textured paint, deep shadows,
> dramatic chiaroscuro, muted earthy palette of antique gold, bronze, dark umber, olive green and
> burnt orange, aged varnish, subtle imperfections, mysterious atmosphere, no text, no border

## A: The Archivist (classic)
> A small, clever monkey scholar with intelligent amber eyes and a weathered pith helmet, crouched on a
> stack of ancient leather-bound tomes in a candlelit underground archive. He studies a single glowing
> trading card through a tarnished brass magnifying glass. Scrolls, wax-sealed letters and dusty
> astrolabes surround him. One warm candle lights him from below, and the vaulted stone darkness behind
> him fades into shadow. The pose is quiet, curious and a little sly.

## B: The Rooftop Thief (pixel)
> A nimble monkey thief in a pith helmet with a long scarf, leaping between moonlit domed rooftops of an
> ancient desert bazaar city at night. He clutches a trading card to his chest and holds a brass
> magnifying glass in his tail. A huge pale crescent moon, lantern-lit alleys below, and drifting smoke.
> The feeling is dynamic, playful and full of mischief.
(I'll draw this one as hand-placed pixel art. This image is only reference, and optional.)

## C: The Relic Seeker (hybrid)
> An old, wise monkey explorer in a battered pith helmet and a bronze-buckled harness, reaching into the
> open chest of a dormant brass clockwork automaton in a ruined temple full of gears and broken
> columns. Inside, a trading card glows with green-gold light that catches his face. Rays of dusty light
> come from a crack in the ceiling. The feeling is reverent, mysterious and slightly eerie.
